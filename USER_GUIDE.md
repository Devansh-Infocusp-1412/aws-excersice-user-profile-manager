# User Guide

This application lets users create an account, verify their email, sign in, and manage their personal profile.

## What You Can Do

- Create a new user account
- Verify your account using a code sent to your email
- Sign in securely
- Update your personal profile details
- Upload a profile image
- Save and review your profile information
- Sign out when finished

## Before You Start

Make sure you have:

- A valid email address
- A username you want to use
- A password you can remember

## Application Flow

### 1. Open the Application

When the application opens, you will see the sign-in screen.

From there, you can:

- Sign in if you already have an account
- Click `Create account` if you are a new user

### 2. Create a New Account

If you are a new user:

1. Click `Create account`.
2. Enter your:
   - Email
   - Username
   - Password
3. Click `Sign Up`.

If the registration is successful, the application will ask you to enter a confirmation code.

### 3. Verify Your Email Address

After registration, a verification code is sent to your email address.

1. Check your inbox for the verification email.
2. Enter the confirmation code in the `Confirmation Code` field.
3. Click `Confirm`.

Important:

- If you do not see the email in your inbox, check your `Spam` or `Junk` folder.
- The verification email may take a short time to arrive.

After successful confirmation, return to the sign-in screen and log in with your username and password.

### 4. Sign In

If you already have an account:

1. Enter your username.
2. Enter your password.
3. Click `Sign In`.

If the credentials are correct, you will be taken to the profile page.

### 5. Complete or Update Your Profile

After signing in, you can manage your profile details.

Available fields include:

- Name
- Gender
- Date of Birth
- Height (cm)
- Profile Image

To update your profile:

1. Enter or edit your information.
2. Upload an image if needed.
3. Click `Save Profile`.

If the save is successful, a success message will appear.

### 6. Upload a Profile Image

To upload your profile picture:

1. Click the file upload field in the `Profile Image` section.
2. Choose an image from your device.
3. Wait for the upload to complete.

After upload:

- The image preview will appear on the page.
- The uploaded image is linked to your profile.

### 7. Sign Out

When you are done:

1. Click `Sign Out` in the top area of the page.

You will be logged out and returned to the login screen.

## Admin User Flow

Some users may have admin access.

Admin users can:

- Sign in normally
- View the `Admin: All Users` section
- See profile information for all users

If you are not an admin, this section will not be shown.

## Common Messages You May See

- `User already exists`
  This means the username has already been registered. Try a different username or sign in with the existing account.

- `Please enter your email`
  The email field is required during account creation.

- `Please enter a username`
  The username field is required.

- `Please enter your password`
  The password field is required.

- `Confirmation failed`
  The verification code may be incorrect or expired. Try entering the code again and re-check the email you received.

- `Sign in failed`
  Your username or password may be incorrect.

## Tips for Users

- Keep your username and password in a safe place.
- Use the same username you created during registration when signing in.
- If your verification email does not appear, always check the `Spam` or `Junk` folder.
- Save your profile after making changes, or the updates may not be stored.

## Quick Summary

1. Open the app
2. Create an account if you are new
3. Check your email for the verification code
4. Also check the spam folder if the email is not in your inbox
5. Confirm your account
6. Sign in
7. Fill in your profile
8. Upload your image
9. Save your profile
10. Sign out when finished
